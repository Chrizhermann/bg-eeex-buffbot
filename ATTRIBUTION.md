## Code

**[BuffBot Redux](https://github.com/zach-hopkins/bg-eeex-buffbot) by Zach Hopkins** —
MIT. Zach's fork contributed the recipient-first planning design, the themed
control layer, improved item and buff discovery, the temporary simulation
fast-forward, the HUD preset picker and the buff search field. Parts of it are
adapted here with his agreement; the two projects exchange ideas and code
freely. Where BuffBot's behaviour differs from Redux, the difference is
deliberate and does not reflect on his work.

**[EEex](https://github.com/Bubb13/EEex) by Bubb** — BuffBot is a consumer of
EEex's Lua API. EEex is not bundled; it is a separate dependency the player
installs.

**[BubbleBuffs](https://github.com/factubsio/BubbleBuffs) by factubsio** — the
original inspiration, from Pathfinder: Wrath of the Righteous. No code is shared.

## Artwork

The themed control art under `buffbot/visual/` — checkbox states, tabs, button
plates, slots, wells, corners, the scrollbar, the search and close icons, the
cast and action icons, the directional arrows and the glyph sheet — comes from
the BuffBot Redux design package by way of Zach Hopkins' fork.

That artwork derives from **Infinity UI++ v1.15 by Pecca**. The directional
arrows are rotations of UI++'s `RGDAROW1`; the plates, checkbox states, corners
and scrollbar were rebuilt from the design package's PNG exports. It is used
here with Pecca's permission, as recorded in that design package.

**Redistribution of this artwork remains subject to that permission.** The MIT
License covers BuffBot's own code and does not extend to it. If you fork or
repackage BuffBot and intend to publish the artwork, obtain your own permission
first, or build the mod without `buffbot/visual/`.

No `RG`-prefixed Infinity UI++ resource is installed or overwritten, and
Infinity UI++ is not required to run BuffBot. BuffBot ships its own art under
private `BFB`-prefixed names and numbered PVRZ pages in the range
88809–88993.

Fonts, spell and item icons and character portraits are referenced from the
player's own game installation and from Infinity UI++ when it is present. None
of them are bundled.

The panel frames and parchment backgrounds (`BFBOTFR*.PVRZ`, `BFBOTBG*.MOS`,
`MOS99xx.PVRZ`) and the action-bar icon (`BFBOTAB.BAM`) are BuffBot's own,
generated by the converters in `tools/`.
