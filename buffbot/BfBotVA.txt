BuffBot Redux visual resources

Frame kit: Infinity UI++ v1.15 by Pecca, adapted through the supplied
Inventory Redux modular renderer and BuffBot Redux design manifests.
Directional arrows: supplied rotations of UI++ RGDAROW1.
Glyphs, layout and tokens: supplied BuffBot Redux design package.

Control plates, checkbox states, popup rails/corners and the scrollbar are
rebuilt from the supplied design PNG exports under private BFB-prefixed BAM
names. This enables all four skins without an Infinity UI++ installation.
No RG-prefixed game or UI++ resource is installed or overwritten.

Optional UI++ fonts, spell/item icons and portraits are referenced by name
from the game installation. Fonts, icons and portraits are not bundled.
Stock NORMAL/STONESML fonts are used when the UI++ fonts are absent.

The design package records the project owner's permission to use Pecca's
artwork. Public redistribution remains subject to that permission.
The original design package contains full provenance and font licenses.

See BfBotVA.json for the exact generated resource allowlist and SHA-256 hashes.
